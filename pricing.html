You are paying monthly for a recurring schedule. HVAC and fridge filters are not replaced every month.

What's covered

  • One standard pleated HVAC filter per covered home, replaced quarterly by default.
  • Complete Care covers one refrigerator and two scheduled filter replacements per year, with a $65 material allowance per replacement.
  • Appointments, reminders, installation, and refill labor for scheduled plan visits.
  • Equipment and filter compatibility confirmed before enrollment.

What needs a separate quote

  • Additional HVAC filters, multiple systems, deep-media filters, and specialty sizes.
  • Fridge filters above the included allowance.
  • Whole-house or other specialized filtration setups.
  • Early replacements beyond scheduled coverage, return trips, and separate callouts.

A separate local callout starts at $39 plus any additional materials. We confirm charges before proceeding. Repairs, diagnostics, and emergency service are outside these plans.

Plan details

A few things to know

How often do you visit?

Air Care includes four scheduled HVAC replacement visits annually. Salt Care, Home Essentials, and Complete Care include monthly softener visits. HVAC and fridge replacements are combined with those visits when due. Equipment requirements may call for a different schedule, which we quote before enrollment.

Are filters really included?

Yes, within the coverage above. We identify the right models before you enroll. Additional or specialty filters are quoted in advance, so you can approve your actual total.

What if a filter warning appears early?

Contact us to review the model and replacement history. An alert does not automatically mean an additional free filter. Any replacement outside scheduled coverage or a separate visit requires an approved charge. We correct our own installation errors at no additional charge.

Can I cancel?

The homeowner plans are month-to-month. Email us before your next renewal to stop future billing and service. The one-time setup fee is separate from the recurring subscription. Final billing dates and service terms are confirmed before enrollment.

Do you serve all of Utah County?

We check your address against available service routes before confirming enrollment. Salt-only service is especially dependent on route availability. Sending an inquiry does not guarantee availability at an address.

What if I don't know my filter size?

Select “Not sure / custom” on the inquiry form. We will help identify your equipment and quote any specialty needs before service begins.

Managing rentals?

Property-manager service is quoted around the homes, equipment, access, and route. Homeowner subscription prices are not automatically applied to a rental portfolio.
